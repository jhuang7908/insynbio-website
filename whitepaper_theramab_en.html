Background and challenges

In early antibody R&D, sequence design and developability judgments are usually spread across several tools and several people, so information is hard to connect. Three problems come up again and again.

1

Humanization and developability are disconnected

Humanization is done in one tool and developability in another; changing one sequence means exporting and re-importing, and the trade-offs of back-mutation and their effect on developability cannot be seen on the same chart.

2

Risk is only numbers, with no sense of position

A single physicochemical parameter lacks a reference, so the team cannot tell whether a value is high or low, nor in which direction to optimize.

3

Formulation and sequence are considered separately

Formulation is only considered after the sequence is fixed; by the time viscosity or aggregation problems appear it is already late, and there is no direct link between risk sites and formulation levers.

Sequences are a client's most core asset. TheraMab makes the three things above a single workflow, while treating privacy and information security as a prerequisite of the product rather than an add-on.

Workflow

From the input sequence to the formulation advice, the client works through one continuous flow, and the result of each step carries over to the next.

StepWhat happens
1 · InputPaste or upload the heavy and light chains (CSV, FASTA, Excel; templates provided). Non-human antibodies go through humanization; fully human or humanized sequences go directly to developability assessment.
2 · HumanizationSeveral framework pairings, each with four back-mutation levels (none, conservative, balanced, aggressive). The client selects the pairings of interest; later assessment is shown only for those.
3 · AssessmentHumanness, physicochemical developability, FlashTAP, and a CMC summary — each parameter shown within its reference range via a position map and radar chart.
4 · OptimizationRisk sites split into CDR and FR tables. FR sites carry substitution suggestions; check them to generate an optimized sequence in one click.
5 · Re-assessmentAn optimized sequence added back is re-assessed automatically, with whole-antibody, CDR-only, and FR-only results, to see whether key parameters improved.
6 · Formulation adviceAdvice tiered for subcutaneous and intravenous routes, formulation codes, and physicochemically similar marketed molecules; each risk-site lever links to a formulation in the knowledge base.
7 · Library & reportCandidate sequences can enter the account library, and a comprehensive report including the formulation advice and its rationale is generated.

Analysis state is saved per job in the client's own browser; "New analysis" clears everything in one click without affecting sequences already in the library.

Core capabilities

Humanization

Two paths in parallel: classic CDR grafting, and a neural-network (NN) design that keeps more of the parent framework. Both use four back-mutation levels and the same rules; they differ in starting point, so NN has fewer positions to consider.

Developability and manufacturability assessment

Physicochemical and structural parameters: instability index, hydrophobicity (GRAVY), chemical risk sites, pI, Fv net charge, the viscosity indicator FvCSP, hydrophobic patches (SAP), and aggregation propensity (A3D). FlashTAP gives a TAP-like rapid risk indicator.

Each parameter is placed in a reference range with "pass, caution, exceeds, falls short" position labels — not a pass/fail judgment from wet-lab work, and not a release criterion. Clients can also enter their own measured values (Tm, expression, HIC, SMAC, AC-SINS, PSR, accelerated-stability slope, BVP), and a knowledge-base page explains each assay.

Risk-site optimization

CDR and FR risk sites are shown separately. CDR sites (which involve binding) get formulation and process hints only; FR sites get specific substitution suggestions, with conservative handling for positions such as conserved tryptophan. Checked suggestions generate an optimized sequence that can be exported as FASTA or re-assessed.

CMC formulation optimization and advice

Tiered advice for subcutaneous and intravenous routes, each with high, medium, and low concentration tiers, giving pH, buffer system, excipients, and viscosity risk. A formulation code and physicochemically similar marketed molecules are provided, and each risk site maps to a formulation lever linked to specific knowledge-base entries.

The formulation-related outputs are suggestions based on rules and surrogate indicators; they are not a viscosity-prediction model and not experimentally validated conclusions.

Privacy and information security

Privacy and information security are a prerequisite of the product. The web edition already implements the measures below; each has been independently reviewed and validated.

AspectMeasure
Data isolationEach client's library and jobs are stored under their own account and invisible to others; accessing another user's job and a non-existent job return the same result; trial examples are shared read-only.
Access controlLogin authentication, accounts opened by invitation; identity is verified by the access layer, client-forged identity headers are discarded, and without a valid identity all writes are rejected.
Algorithm & database confidentialityPages and interfaces do not show source names, database names, sample sizes, or citations; external outputs drop internal fields and use neutral codes; scripts are minified; public interfaces are rate-limited.
Network securitySite-wide HTTPS, with HSTS, security headers, hidden server version, rate limiting, and request-body size limits; old direct-connect ports are closed.
Client-side stateAnalysis state and drafts are kept only in the client's own browser and are not uploaded; "New analysis" clears them in one click.
Backup & recoveryDaily consistency backups of the library and account information, weekly recovery tests, and health checks and alerts.
Audit & change controlEvery release is independently reviewed, accepted in an isolated environment, and independently verified by another person, with a rollback method kept ready.

Data note: the platform stores the sequences, computed results, and library entries the client submits; only the client's own account can see them, and they are kept until deletion is requested, with at most about two more months retained in backups.

Boundaries, stated plainly: we currently use account-level data isolation and access control, without third-party security certification or an independent penetration test; backups are currently on the same host, with no off-site copy yet; operations staff can read private data files at the server level. For highly sensitive sequences, we recommend the locally run desktop edition.

Scope and limitations

This is a reference tool for scientific research and early R&D; the following boundaries should be known in advance.

Position labels are not experimental results

"Pass, caution, exceeds, falls short" indicate where a candidate sits within the reference range, not wet-lab success/failure, and not a release condition. We recommend a same-condition comparison against a clinical antibody.

Formulation is rule-based advice

Based on rules and surrogate indicators, not a viscosity-prediction model, and needs experimental validation.

Humanness comparison for external sequences has gaps

For fully human or externally designed sequences there is no OASis humanness score; without a parent sequence, comparison to the parent (loops, H3, Vernier, interface, back-mutation) is shown as not assessed.

Unsupported formats and scale

Single-chain antibodies (scFv) and nanobodies (VHH) are not currently supported. Up to 12 pairs per run; the assessment interface allows at most 15 calls per 5 minutes.

This product is for scientific research and early R&D reference and is not a basis for clinical decisions, diagnosis, treatment, or regulatory submissions. Before any candidate enters development, it needs an independent judgment by qualified personnel together with experiments and regulatory requirements.
