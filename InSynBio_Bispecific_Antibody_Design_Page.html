Bispecific Format Selection

Despite dozens of described bispecific architectures, clinical approvals concentrate in a handful of formats. The real decision is not which large format to use — it is which heterodimerization strategy within the IgG-like framework best suits your targets, LC compatibility, and production platform.

SmartLink™ Optimization

Data-driven linker selection, not guesswork. We screen your design against our library of clinical linkers.

  • Geometry Matching — Selects linker length (G4S, EAAAK, etc.) based on the precise distance between epitopes.
  • Co-folding Validation — AlphaFold2 Multimer modeling of the ternary complex (Ab + Ag1 + Ag2) to verify simultaneous binding.
  • Flexibility Scoring — Balances entropic penalty (too flexible) vs. steric hindrance (too rigid).

Virtual CMC

Solve chain mispairing before wet-lab production. Essential for IgG-like formats (CrossMab, KiH). InSynBio applies a three-layer mispairing audit: HC selectivity → LC mispairing → VH/VL domain crossover.

  • HC Selectivity — KiH, DuoBody DEKK, DD/KK — evaluated and recommended based on target/format.
  • LC Mispairing — CrossMab / Common Light Chain compatibility assessment.
  • VH/VL Pairing Stability (VPSA) — Proprietary assessment of variable domain crossover risk using packing angle, interface BSA, charge complementarity, and mispairing ΔΔG. C-domain engineering alone does not assess this. See
  • Developability Check — Full-spectrum assessment of aggregation (SAP), viscosity, and chemical stability.

Faricimab VPSA: Arm-Specific Selectivity in KiH+CrossFab Co-Expression

CrossFab addresses LC-level mispairing — but the Fv variable domain interface is a separate layer. InSynBio VPSA analysis of Faricimab (VEGF-A×ANG-2, FDA approved 2022) revealed that Arm A (VH3/Vκ) has strong intrinsic Fv selectivity (156 native salt bridges → 43 in crossover, −72%), while Arm B (VH1/Vλ) relies primarily on CrossFab C-domain architecture. BSA alone would give the opposite conclusion — demonstrating why multi-metric VPSA is required.

Common Questions

What materials do I need to provide for a bispecific antibody design project?

Simply provide the amino acid sequences (VH/VL or VHH) for both arms and target information. If you already have monospecific antibodies, they can be used as the starting point for bispecific conversion.

How is the most suitable bispecific format selected?

Format selection depends on the nature of the targets, required effector functions, and clinical stage. We perform format matching based on our 125+ clinical bispecific database and provide selection rationale with comparative analysis in the report.

How is my sequence data kept confidential?

An NDA process is triggered upon submission. Your sequences and project information are encrypted in transit, not shared with third parties, and raw data is not retained in our systems after project completion.
