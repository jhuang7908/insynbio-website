Vaccine Design Knowledge Base

Ranked antigen evidence across tumor, infectious, and tolerogenic tracks — with TCR co-stimulation data, multi-epitope mRNA logic, delivery platform selection, HLA population coverage, and benchmark dry-wet learning patterns. Each record carries explicit evidence tier, verification status, and machine-readable justification.

All records are sourced from public peer-reviewed literature, clinical trial reports, and curated databases (PDB, IEDB, UniProt). Evidence tier and verification status are displayed for each entry.
