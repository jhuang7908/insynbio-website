Affinity Maturation Computational Tool Benchmark Study

Based on 854 real pre-clinical antibody mutation data points, this study reveals a systematic negative correlation between traditional rigid-body physical scoring and true affinity — and proposes an AI-MD multi-scale sequential filtering solution.

Background: Standard computational virtual affinity maturation (VAM) pipelines rely on rigid-body physical energy scoring to rank mutants, then send the Top-K candidates to experimental validation. In practice, real-world hit rates from this approach are often disappointing.

Research Objective: Systematically evaluate multiple categories of computational tools on a large-scale real antibody mutation dataset, define the effective usage boundaries of each tool, and establish a more reliable screening decision framework.

🔬 Physical Root Cause: Rigid Backbone Assumption Systematically Penalizes Induced Fit

When bulkier high-affinity side chains (aromatic, strongly charged residues) are introduced, rigid-body scoring software assigns extreme penalties for "steric clash." In real solution, minor backbone and side-chain relaxation (induced fit) readily accommodates these mutations. This causes the most valuable mutations to be systematically eliminated, with extremely high false-negative rates.

Sequential Filtering Validation: Predictable Hit Rate Improvement

✓ Key Insight: Physics Tools Should Be Used "In Reverse" for Lead Optimization

After initial filtering by AI naturalness and thermodynamic stability, applying rigid-body physics in the forward direction (selecting the lowest 25% energy) drops hit rate from 78.5% to 69.1%. Using physics tools in reverse (selecting the side with poorer rigid-body scores, since high-affinity mutations tend to score worse on rigid-body evaluation) raises hit rate to 82.4%.

InSynBio Multi-Scale Cascade Engine

Based on this benchmark, InSynBio has fully redesigned its affinity maturation screening architecture — replacing the "energy-score-only" approach with a multi-stage, functionally stratified sequential filtering system:

0
Hotspot Mapping (Alanine Scan)
Use physics tools for "loss-of-function" prediction — their highest-accuracy scenario. Determine which positions can be mutated and which must not be touched.
Define mutation space
1
AI Dimensionality Reduction (Naturalness & Stability Gate)
Deep-learning language models and thermodynamic stability prediction to eliminate unnatural and unstable sequences. Millisecond computation, extremely high throughput.
10,000+ → ~3,000
2
Rigid-Body Mine-Sweeping (Reverse Physics)
Used only for "loss-of-function" detection — eliminate absolute non-binders causing severe steric/electrostatic conflicts. Never use for positive ranking.
~3,000 → ~500
3
3D AI Positive Selection (3D Microenvironment Probability)
Antibody-specific 3D inverse folding AI models for positive ranking at the structural microenvironment probability level — implicitly capturing flexibility and solvation effects.
~500 → Top 150
4
Clustering & CMC Pre-Screen (Diversity & CMC Gate)
Ensure mutational strategy diversity (avoid homogenization) and proactively eliminate candidates with excessive aggregation risk or pI shift.
Top 150 → ~50
5
High-Precision MD Validation
Full-atom force field + implicit solvent molecular dynamics minimization on only ~50 candidates. The only method that truly reflects side-chain relaxation and solvation — and the only valid standard for judging "better than wild-type."
→ Top 10 delivered

Project Decision Rules

Prohibited Actions
✗ Using rigid-body physics for absolute Top-K ranking of lead optimization mutants
✗ Using contact counting in de novo design scenarios
✗ Replacing diversity coverage with computational scoring for high binder-rate (>70%) targets
Recommended Practices
✓ Physics tools only for Ala Scan hotspot mapping and absolute non-binder elimination
✓ Classify target type (structure-constrained vs. sequence-tolerant) before selecting tool strategy
✓ Final "better than wild-type" judgment must be based on high-precision MD energy
Conclusion: Different Tools Answer Different Physical Questions — They Cannot Be Mixed
Rigid-Body Physics
Answers: "Which mutations break binding?"

(Only reliable final verdict)
