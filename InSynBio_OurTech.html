Tech Platform

AbEngineCore — Unified Design Engine

AbEngineCore is our core computational framework, integrating 30+ industry-leading biocatalysis and AI modeling tools. We organicially combine machine learning with 1,142 clinical antibody benchmarks to deliver executable designs. Here are 8 core technology components commonly used in our workflows:

Structure Prediction

AlphaFold2

High-precision protein structure prediction for antibody variable regions and complex modeling, providing the structural basis for developability and immunogenicity assessment.

Antibody Structure

ABodyBuilder2

Specialized antibody structure prediction model optimized for Fv regions, with CDR conformation accuracy superior to general structure prediction tools.

Refinement

HADDOCK3

Data-driven protein-protein docking and interface refinement for complex analysis and affinity maturation.

Sequence Design

ProteinMPNN

Deep learning-driven inverse folding for de novo CDR design and framework optimization.

Diffusion

RFdiffusion

Diffusion-based scaffold design for epitope-targeted de novo antibody generation.

Annotation

ANARCI

Deep learning antibody numbering system supporting Kabat, IMGT, and Chothia schemes, precisely defining FR and CDR regions.

Immunogenicity

IEDB API

MHC-II binding prediction covering 27 HLA-DR alleles for T-cell epitope risk scoring based on IEDB.

Stability

ThermoMPNN

Mutation thermal stability prediction for screening affinity maturation candidates combined with developability analysis.
