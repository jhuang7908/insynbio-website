Background

A 30-Year Clinical Benchmark

muMAb4D5 is a murine monoclonal antibody targeting the human HER2 receptor. Its humanized descendant, trastuzumab (Herceptin®), has been the gold standard for HER2-positive breast cancer treatment for over 28 years — generating ~$10 billion in annual sales and treating millions of patients worldwide.

From Mouse Antibody to Global Blockbuster

1992
Carter et al. (PNAS) — First CDR-grafting humanization of muMAb4D5. Multiple variants tested; 5 VH back-mutations required to restore affinity.
1998
FDA Approval — humAb4D5-8 approved as trastuzumab (Herceptin®). A landmark in modern biologics.
2000–2020s
Humanization matures — CDR definition standardized, Vernier zone principles established, computational tools refined.
2026
This project — InSynBio AbEngineCore V4.5 applies a fully systematic, ML-informed approach to humanize muMAb4D5 from scratch.

Design Decisions

Vernier Zone Optimization

Vernier zone residues are framework positions that directly influence CDR geometry. Back-mutations restore murine amino acids at structurally critical sites. Our analysis — informed by SASA calculations and contact maps — identified just 4 positions in VH, and none in VL.
