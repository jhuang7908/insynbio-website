FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about our services, workflow, and deliverables.

What information do I need to provide to start a project? +

For antibody humanization or VHH design: VH/VL (or VHH) amino acid sequences in FASTA format, target antigen name, and any known affinity data. For CMC assessment: sequence files only. We will confirm the scope and timeline after reviewing your input.

How long does a typical project take? +

Standard deliverables are ready in 3–5 business days. Complex projects (e.g., full humanization + CMC + structure prediction + immunogenicity) may require 7–10 business days. Rush delivery is available upon request.

What does the final report include? +

Every report includes: final designed sequences (FASTA), full rationale for each design decision, structure prediction results (RMSD, VH/VL angle), CMC developability metrics benchmarked against clinical references, immunogenicity risk assessment, and an executive summary suitable for stakeholder review. PDF and markdown formats are provided.

Do you offer wet lab validation? +

Our core service is computational (dry lab). We partner with CRO laboratories for expression, binding assays (SPR/BLI), and stability testing. We can coordinate CRO handoff as part of the project scope — please mention this in your inquiry.

Is my sequence data kept confidential? +

Yes. All submitted sequences and project data are treated as strictly confidential. We are happy to sign an NDA prior to project initiation. Data is used solely for the purposes of the contracted project and is not shared with third parties.

What AI tools and databases does InSynBio use? +

Our AbEngineCore platform integrates: deep-learning antibody numbering (ANARCII), structure prediction (AlphaFold2, ABodyBuilder2, HADDOCK3), de novo sequence design (ProteinMPNN, RFdiffusion), a curated 1,142-antibody clinical germline database, CMC benchmarking against AbRef-458, and MHC-II immunogenicity prediction via IEDB API-based prediction EL mode (IEDB API, 27 HLA-DR alleles).
