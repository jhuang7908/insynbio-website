AI-Assisted Biologics R&D Decision Support
Solving problems, not just showcasing tools

InSynBio integrates AI Analysis + 6 Proprietary Clinical Databases + Expert Judgment to provide end-to-end decision support from sequence to report for antibody, bispecific, CAR-T, and vaccine development teams—systematically identifying and mitigating R&D failure risks before entering the wet lab.

CMC Developability Gap
CMC boundaries are not considered during sequence optimization, exposing gaps only at the IND stage, leading to ~30% of candidates failing pre-clinically.
Cost: 6–18 months of wasted development cycles
Delayed Immunogenicity Risk
Inadequate humanization and unscreened T-cell epitopes trigger ADA signals only during animal trials, forcing a complete restart.
Cost: Failed animal trials = million-dollar reinvestment
Format Selection Relies on Experience
Selection of bispecific / ADC / CAR components lacks clinical data support, leading to poor reproducibility and unquantifiable risks when taking the wrong direction.
Cost: Format errors mean redoing the entire pipeline

Our Solution: Intervene before the wet lab—using 25+ AI tools for rapid analysis, 6 clinical databases for format-specific benchmarking, and expert judgment for actionable recommendations. Not just scores, but directions. Not just tools, but a moat.
