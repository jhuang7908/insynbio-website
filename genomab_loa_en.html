Letter of Authorization

Ref No.: GBI-LOA-2026-001  |  Effective: March 1, 2026
Date: March 1, 2026 Re: Appointment of China Industrialization Advisor To: Professor Zhimou Xue

To Whom It May Concern,

This Letter of Authorization is issued by Genomab Biotech Inc. ("the Company"), a biotechnology company incorporated in the State of New York, United States of America, with its principal place of business at 423 W 127th St, New York, NY 10027. The Company focuses on humanized immune system models, antibody engineering, and AI-driven drug design platforms for next-generation biotherapeutics.

1. Appointment

The Company hereby formally appoints Professor Zhimou Xue as the China Industrialization Advisor of Genomab Biotech Inc., effective March 1, 2026.

2. Scope of Authorization

  • 1
    Industrial Strategy Development Provide strategic consulting for the commercialization of humanized immune system model platforms and related technologies in China.
  • 2
    Partnership & Collaboration Development Facilitate introductions and collaborations with academic institutions, research organizations, pharmaceutical companies, and CRO platforms in China.
  • 3
    Technology Translation & Commercialization Support the application and commercial deployment of the following technology areas:
    • Humanized immune system animal models • Companion animal therapeutics (including pet antibody drug development)
  • 4
    Ecosystem Development Assist in building an industry network and identifying application scenarios around the Company's core platforms.

3. Limitations

  • This authorization is advisory in nature only and does not create an employment or labor relationship.
  • Professor Zhimou Xue is not authorized to execute contracts, make financial commitments, or assume legal obligations on behalf of the Company without prior written approval.
  • All commercial agreements and major business decisions require formal approval by Genomab Biotech Inc.

4. Term

This authorization is effective from March 1, 2026 and shall remain in effect through February 28, 2027, unless earlier terminated by written notice. The parties may mutually agree to renew upon expiration.

5. Purpose

This Letter is issued to facilitate academic exchanges, industry partnership discussions, and relevant external communications. It does not constitute or replace a legally binding contract.
