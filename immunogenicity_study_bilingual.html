Anti-drug antibodies (ADAs) are a primary clinical risk for biologics — they can reduce efficacy, alter pharmacokinetics, and in rare cases trigger serious adverse events. ADA rates vary enormously across antibodies, from 0% to over 87% in our dataset, making prediction genuinely difficult.

This study applies InSynBio's AbEngineCore Immunogenicity Module to 138 therapeutic antibodies with documented clinical ADA rates. For each molecule, 57+ sequence, structural, glycosylation, and clinical features were extracted — including novel interaction terms — to build stratified predictive scoring models, validated by strict Leave-One-Out (LOO) cross-validation.

Biological Interpretation

1 · 1 · The Immune-Depleting Target Paradox

，（CD52, CD4, CD25） ADA 。 ADA ，（DAMPs），，。Alemtuzumab（ CD52，ADA = 85%）。 Antibodies targeting immune-depleting antigens (CD52, CD4, CD25) paradoxically show higher ADA rates. Although they deplete the very cells responsible for ADA, target-mediated cytolysis releases massive quantities of intracellular antigen and DAMPs, triggering robust secondary immune activation that overrides transient lymphopenia. Alemtuzumab (anti-CD52, ADA = 85%) is the archetypal example.

2 · CDR-H3 2 · CDR-H3 Aromaticity and Immune Tolerance

，CDR-H3 （Trp, Tyr, Phe） ADA （r = −0.324）。：，， MHC-II ； CDR-H3 ，。 CDR-H3 aromatic content (Trp, Tyr, Phe) inversely correlates with ADA rates (r = −0.324). One hypothesis: aromatic residues form stable antigen-binding contacts, reducing conformational flexibility and limiting proteolytic processing and MHC-II loading efficiency. Alternatively, aromatic-enriched CDR-H3 loops may resemble endogenous immunoglobulin architecture, activating clonal anergy.

3 · T 3 · T-Cell Epitope Clustering in Fully Human Antibodies

MHC-II （r = +0.322） ADA （n = 54）。 T B ，。 CDR ：MHC-II 。 The number of strong MHC-II binders (r = +0.322) and epitope clusters are the strongest predictors in the fully human group (n = 54). Clustered epitopes increase productive T-cell:B-cell cognate interactions. This finding supports CDR engineering strategies aimed at dispersing rather than eliminating MHC-II binding peptides.

4 · ADA 4 · Assay Technology as a Confound

（r = +0.281）（r = +0.259）。：（ECL） ELISA ADA， ADA ，。 Approval era (r = +0.281) and assay generation (r = +0.259) positively correlate with ADA. This reflects a well-known systematic bias: modern drug-tolerant and ECL assays detect lower-titer ADAs missed by older ELISA platforms, inflating reported ADA rates for recently approved antibodies regardless of their intrinsic immunogenicity.

5 · 5 · Minimal Models Outperform Complex Ones

138 ，3–5 LOO 。 n = 54–84 ，-， GBR 。 Across all tested configurations on the 138-antibody panel, 3–5 feature models consistently outperform more complex ones in LOO cross-validation. With n = 54–84, the bias-variance tradeoff still strongly favors parsimony, but non-linear GBR models (for the FH group) can now capture interaction effects with statistically significant confidence.
