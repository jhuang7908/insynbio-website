01

研究概述Overview

抗药抗体（ADA）是生物制剂的主要临床风险之一，可导致疗效下降、药代动力学改变，严重时引发不良反应。然而，不同抗体的 ADA 发生率差异极大——在我们的数据集中从 0% 到 85% 以上，使得预测工作极具挑战性。

本研究将 InSynBio AbEngineCore 免疫原性模块应用于 138 个拥有临床 ADA 文献记录的治疗性抗体。对每个抗体提取 57+ 个序列、结构、糖基化及临床特征（含新发现的交互项），构建分层预测评分模型，并通过严格的留一法（LOO）交叉验证进行评估。

Anti-drug antibodies (ADAs) are a primary clinical risk for biologics — they can reduce efficacy, alter pharmacokinetics, and in rare cases trigger serious adverse events. ADA rates vary enormously across antibodies, from 0% to over 87% in our dataset, making prediction genuinely difficult.

This study applies InSynBio's AbEngineCore Immunogenicity Module to 138 therapeutic antibodies with documented clinical ADA rates. For each molecule, 57+ sequence, structural, glycosylation, and clinical features were extracted — including novel interaction terms — to build stratified predictive scoring models, validated by strict Leave-One-Out (LOO) cross-validation.

为何分层建模？Why Stratified Modeling?

将 138 个抗体混合建模时，任何单个特征与 ADA 率的最高 Spearman 相关性仅为 r ≤ 0.19。这是预期的结果：人源化与全人源抗体的免疫原性驱动因素在机制上截然不同。

When all 138 antibodies are pooled, the best achievable Spearman correlation is modest (r ≤ 0.19). This is expected: humanized and fully human antibodies are immunogenic for fundamentally different reasons.

人源化抗体（n = 84） Humanized Antibodies (n = 84)

ADA 风险主要由临床与药理环境驱动：联合免疫抑制用药 × 半衰期交互项（r = −0.20），检测法代际效应。残留的鼠源 CDR 骨架创造了一个基础免疫原性风险，随后被患者管理因素所调节。注：41 个新增人源化抗体缺少完整的混杂因素数据，造成信号衰减。 ADA risk is driven by clinical and pharmacological context: co-medication × half-life interaction (r = −0.20), assay generation. Residual murine CDR scaffolds create baseline immunogenic risk, modulated by patient management.

全人源抗体（n = 54） Fully Human Antibodies (n = 54)

使用人源序列框架，ADA 风险更具本征性——由强 MHC-II 结合肽计数（r = +0.32）、表位聚集和 VH 生殖系外源性驱动。与 n=27 相比，n 翻倍至 54 后统计置信度显著提升。 With human-sequence frameworks, ADA risk is more intrinsic — driven by strong MHC-II binder count (r = +0.32), epitope clustering, and VH germline foreignness. With n doubled from 27 to 54, statistical confidence substantially improves.

数据完整性发现：Data completeness insight: 人源化组信号衰减（LOO r: 0.39 → 0.25，随 n 从 43 增至 84）直接量化了记录临床混杂因素的价值。这是优先补全缺失数据的最强理由。 The attenuation of HU signal (LOO r: 0.39 → 0.25 as n grows from 43 to 84) directly quantifies the value of recording clinical confounders. This is the strongest argument for completing missing confounder annotations.

生物学解读Biological Interpretation

1 · 免疫耗竭靶点悖论1 · The Immune-Depleting Target Paradox

在人源化组中，靶向免疫耗竭型抗原（CD52, CD4, CD25）的抗体呈现出悖论性的更高 ADA 率。尽管此类药物耗竭了负责 ADA 的免疫细胞，但靶细胞的大量裂解会释放细胞内抗原和危险相关分子模式（DAMPs），触发强烈的继发性免疫反应，进而克服了短暂的淋巴细胞减少症。Alemtuzumab（抗 CD52，ADA = 85%）是典型案例。 Antibodies targeting immune-depleting antigens (CD52, CD4, CD25) paradoxically show higher ADA rates. Although they deplete the very cells responsible for ADA, target-mediated cytolysis releases massive quantities of intracellular antigen and DAMPs, triggering robust secondary immune activation that overrides transient lymphopenia. Alemtuzumab (anti-CD52, ADA = 85%) is the archetypal example.

2 · CDR-H3 芳香族与免疫耐受2 · CDR-H3 Aromaticity and Immune Tolerance

在人源化组中，CDR-H3 芳香族含量（Trp, Tyr, Phe）与 ADA 率呈负相关（r = −0.324）。一种机制假说：芳香族残基形成能量稳定的抗原结合接触，降低构象柔性，从而减少蛋白水解加工和 MHC-II 装载效率；或芳香族富集的 CDR-H3 环与内源性免疫球蛋白的结构更为相似，激活克隆无应答通路。 CDR-H3 aromatic content (Trp, Tyr, Phe) inversely correlates with ADA rates (r = −0.324). One hypothesis: aromatic residues form stable antigen-binding contacts, reducing conformational flexibility and limiting proteolytic processing and MHC-II loading efficiency. Alternatively, aromatic-enriched CDR-H3 loops may resemble endogenous immunoglobulin architecture, activating clonal anergy.

3 · 全人源抗体中的 T 细胞表位聚集3 · T-Cell Epitope Clustering in Fully Human Antibodies

强 MHC-II 结合肽计数（r = +0.322）和表位聚集簇数是全人源组中最强的 ADA 预测因子（n = 54）。空间聚集的表位增加了 T 细胞与 B 细胞有效协作的概率，尤其是当多个高亲和力表位位于同一个抗原处理片段内时。此发现支持的 CDR 序列工程策略是：分散而非消除MHC-II 结合表位。 The number of strong MHC-II binders (r = +0.322) and epitope clusters are the strongest predictors in the fully human group (n = 54). Clustered epitopes increase productive T-cell:B-cell cognate interactions. This finding supports CDR engineering strategies aimed at dispersing rather than eliminating MHC-II binding peptides.

4 · ADA 检测技术的混杂效应4 · Assay Technology as a Confound

批准年代（r = +0.281）和检测平台世代（r = +0.259）在人源化组呈正相关。这反映了一个已知的系统性偏差：现代药物耐受型和电化学发光（ECL）检测平台能检测到旧式 ELISA 无法检测的低滴度 ADA，导致近年批准抗体的 ADA 率报告偏高，与分子本身的内在免疫原性无关。 Approval era (r = +0.281) and assay generation (r = +0.259) positively correlate with ADA. This reflects a well-known systematic bias: modern drug-tolerant and ECL assays detect lower-titer ADAs missed by older ELISA platforms, inflating reported ADA rates for recently approved antibodies regardless of their intrinsic immunogenicity.

5 · 极简模型优于复杂模型5 · Minimal Models Outperform Complex Ones

在 138 抗体面板的所有测试配置中，3–5 特征模型在 LOO 交叉验证中一致优于更复杂的模型。在 n = 54–84 的规模下，偏差-方差权衡仍然强烈偏向简洁性，但非线性 GBR 模型（针对全人源组）现在能够以统计显著的置信度捕获交互效应。 Across all tested configurations on the 138-antibody panel, 3–5 feature models consistently outperform more complex ones in LOO cross-validation. With n = 54–84, the bias-variance tradeoff still strongly favors parsimony, but non-linear GBR models (for the FH group) can now capture interaction effects with statistically significant confidence.

局限性与未来路线图Limitations & Future Roadmap

如何使用本模型：How to use these models: 评分公式应作为相对风险分层工具，而非精确的临床 ADA 率预测器。最适合在同一项目（相同靶点、适应症、分子格式）内对候选分子进行排序，而非预测具体的 ADA 百分比。 Scoring formulas should be interpreted as relative risk stratification tools, not absolute ADA rate predictors. Best applied to rank candidate molecules within the same project (same target, indication, format).

当前局限性Current Limitations

  • 样本量有限：Sample size: 54 个全人源和 84 个人源化抗体支持 3–5 特征模型，但子组分析仍有统计功效不足的风险。 54 fully human and 84 humanized antibodies support 3–5 feature models; subgroup analyses remain underpowered.
  • ADA 数据异质性：ADA data heterogeneity: 不同临床试验的检测方法、cut-off、随访时长差异大，引入显著噪音。 ADA rates pooled across trials with different assay sensitivities, cut-points, and follow-up durations.
  • 缺失因素：Missing factors: 既往 ADA（特别是抗 PEG 抗体）和辅料佐剂效应数据不可得。 Pre-existing ADA (anti-PEG) and excipient adjuvancy data unavailable.
  • 线性假设：Linearity assumption: 加权线性评分无法捕获非线性特征交互。 Weighted additive scores cannot capture non-linear feature interactions.
  • 3D 结构覆盖：3D structural coverage: VL 表面亲水性（r = −0.409）因结构缺失未进入最终模型。 VL surface hydrophilic fraction (r = −0.409) excluded from final model due to incomplete structure availability.
