Platform

The Antibody Developability Assessment platform provides computational analysis, evaluation, gene-engineering humanization, and structure-driven affinity maturation for antibody drug discovery and development. We primarily work from sequences—submit VH/VL or VHH sequences online; we can model structures in-house when needed. Our experts deliver structured reports covering numbering, germline retrieval, developability, immunogenicity, structure assessment, interface analysis, humanization design, and affinity maturation.

All services are expert-reviewed and delivered offline—typically within 3–5 business days upon project confirmation. Our pipeline is based on 1,142 clinical antibody sequences and uses transparent metrics. Humanization employs structure-driven back-mutations and prioritizes germlines from clinical antibodies. Structure assessment is included for all humanization deliverables.

Developability

Developability and CMC

  • Physicochemical — pI, GRAVY, instability index, net charge
  • SAP — 9-mer hydrophobic and 7-mer charge aggregation propensity
  • CDR chemical risk — Deamidation (NG/NS), oxidation (M/W), glycosylation (NxS/T), free Cys
  • TAP — Total CDR length, PSH, PPC, PNC, SFvCSP (InSynBio in-house implementation with reference thresholds). Requires PDB and CDR sequences.
  • CMC design — pI adjustment, FR-only mutation suggestions

Input: VH/VL sequences. For TAP: PDB and CDR sequences are required.

Immunogenicity

Immunogenicity Assessment — Full Report Only

Immunogenicity scoring requires a complete, fully humanized or fully human sequence. It is not provided as a preliminary pre-screen. For early-stage sequences (pre-humanization), only sequence-level chemical stability flags (NG/DG motifs, Met/Trp oxidation, free Cys) are reported. Full immunogenicity assessment is included in the post-humanization Full Report.

  • MHC-II T-cell epitope prediction — InSynBio immunogenicity pipeline (via IEDB API), covering 27 HLA-DR alleles; framework surface risk assessment
  • Risk clustering — Agglomerative clustering of high-risk peptide positions along the sequence
  • Surface immunogenicity — Parker hydrophilicity scoring; SASA-based exposure analysis (when PDB structure is provided)
  • Germline tolerance — IGHV/IGKV/IGLV frequency-weighted filter; germline identity % reported

Input: Complete VH/VL humanized or fully human sequences (PDB optional). Model calibrated on 70 clinical antibodies (LOO r = 0.39–0.52). Results are stratified risk scores, not absolute ADA rate predictions.

Structure

Structure and Interface

  • Whole antibody (Fab) assessment — 13-parameter evaluation: VH-VL angle, interface contacts, Vernier SASA, CDR conformation, pLDDT
  • VHH assessment — Single-domain structure metrics, CDR conformation, pLDDT
  • Antigen-antibody complex assessment — Antibody-antigen interface: BSA, paratope/epitope residues, H-bonds, salt bridges, hydrophobic contacts
  • Multi-antibody comparison — Epitope overlap, competition analysis, BSA/SC comparison (on request)

Input: PDB (Fab, VHH, or antigen-antibody complex). Antigen chain ID required for interface analysis.

Affinity Maturation

Structure-Driven Affinity Maturation

Based on existing complex structures. No de novo structure generation required. Structure assessment included for all deliverables.

  • Antigen-antibody affinity maturation — CDR redesign on Ab-Ag complex backbone for improved binding
  • Ligand-receptor affinity maturation — Paratope optimization on ligand-receptor complex for enhanced affinity
  • VHH affinity maturation — CDR optimization on VHH-antigen complex for single-domain antibodies

Input: PDB of existing complex (Ab-Ag, ligand-receptor, or VHH-antigen). Design region (e.g., CDR-H3) specified per project.

Case Study: muMAb4D5 → Trastuzumab Precursor

The AbEngineCore V4.5.1 pipeline doesn't just apply CDR grafting — it systematically reasons through each engineering decision. Below is the full assessment and humanization trace for muMAb4D5, the mouse anti-HER2 antibody that became the precursor to trastuzumab (Herceptin®).

Input   Antibody: muMAb4D5  Type: Mouse IgG1 (VH/VL)  Target: HER2-ECD Domain IV  Goal: Humanization
A1  Sequence Analysis
IMGT/Chothia numbering, CDR annotation, germline identification?
6 CDRs annotated · IGHV3-23 (VH: 74.1% id) · IGKV1-39 (VL: 84.2% id)
CDR-H3 = 14 residues (long) — critical for HER2-ECD epitope contact. VH/VL co-occurrence in 842-antibody clinical database confirms IGHV3-23 × IGKV1-39 is a high-frequency pairing (top-5%). All 6 CDRs are chemically clean — no deamidation (NG/NS), glycosylation (NxS/T), or oxidation (Met/Trp) risk flags.
A2  Developability Assessment
pI, aggregation risk, CDR chemical flags?
pI 9.1 (murine) · GRAVY −0.21 · 0 CDR chemical flags · SAP alert in FR3
Elevated pI (9.1) increases electrostatic aggregation risk at physiological conditions — CMC alert raised. Hydrophobic patch (SAP) in FR3 contributed by murine-specific residues. No CDR liabilities detected. Humanization is expected to naturally resolve both flags via framework substitution.
A3  Immunogenicity Assessment
MHC-II T-cell epitope risk in framework and CDR regions?
3 high-risk MHC-II clusters in VH-FR2 and VL-FR3 (murine-specific residues) · CDRs clean
InSynBio immunogenicity pipeline (27 HLA-DR alleles) identifies 3 murine-specific FR residue clusters as high-risk T-cell epitopes. All risks reside in framework — CDR-H1/H2/H3 and CDR-L1/L2/L3 are immunogenically clean. Germline tolerance filter confirms IGHV3-23*01 + IGKV1-39*01 as lowest-risk framework pair for this CDR set.
A4  Framework Selection
Which human germline best preserves CDR conformation and minimizes immunogenicity?
IGHV3-23*01 × IGKV1-39*01 — ranked #1 from 842-antibody clinical database
IGHV3-23*01 (VH): most prevalent family (>30% of clinical antibodies); optimal CDR-H1/H2 canonical class match and Vernier profile for CDR-H3 length 14 aa. IGKV1-39*01 (VL): top-5% pairing frequency; perfect Vernier compatibility — zero VL back-mutations needed. ✘ IGHV3-66 rejected: murine parent, high immunogenicity. ✘ IGHV1-69 rejected: incompatible Vernier geometry for CDR-H3 = 14 aa.
A5  CDR Grafting + Vernier Back-mutations
Which positions are structurally critical? Minimum back-mutations to restore VH-VL packing?
4 back-mutations in VH: H49 (S→A), H71 (R→A), H73 (N→T), H78 (L→A) · VL: none required
SASA + contact-map analysis identifies 4 Vernier zone positions requiring back-mutation: H49 (buried non-polar required), H71 (charge disruption risk), H73 (VH core packing), H78 (steric clash prevention). VL: IGKV1-39*01 Vernier profile is fully compatible — zero VL back-mutations needed. ABodyBuilder2 structural validation: VH-VL packing angle deviation = 0.64° from murine reference.
A6  Post-humanization CMC Gate
Does humanization resolve the pI flag? New aggregation or stability risks?
pI 7.86 (resolved) · GRAVY −0.285 · FR3 SAP patch eliminated · 14/15 CMC parameters passed
pI improved from 9.1 → 7.86 — colloidal stability risk resolved by IGHV3-23 framework substitution. FR3 hydrophobic patch eliminated. Zero new deamidation or glycosylation motifs introduced by back-mutations. CMC gate passed against AbRef-458 clinical database benchmarks. Zero free cysteines, zero unpaired disulfides.
A7  Deliverable & Independent Validation
Final sequences, report, and benchmarking against clinical trastuzumab?
hz4D5 VH + VL delivered · 4 back-mutations · 100% CDR identity with clinical Herceptin sequence
Final humanized sequences achieve 100% CDR identity with the published trastuzumab (Herceptin®) sequence — independently validating the AbEngineCore V4.5.1 approach with 73% fewer back-mutations than the original Carter et al. (1992) humanization. Deliverables: annotated FASTA, Vernier rationale table, ABodyBuilder2 structure overlay, and full CMC report. View full report →
