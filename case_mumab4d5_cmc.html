Competitive Advantage

3 Dimensions Better than Herceptin®

On immunogenicity safety and chemical degradation risk, the optimized design outperforms the 28-year clinical gold standard.

T-Cell Epitope Risk
0.61 vs Herceptin 0.65

Lower TCIA score indicates reduced probability of triggering anti-drug antibody (ADA) response in patients. Lower is safer.

High-Risk Epitope Count
21 vs Herceptin 26

5 fewer high-risk MHC-II epitope regions. Herceptin has a reported clinical HAHA rate of 1–6%; this design is predicted to be lower.

Deamidation Sites
1 vs Herceptin 2

Reduced from 2 to 1 via VH84 N→Q. Fewer spontaneous chemical modifications during long-term storage — extending shelf-life stability.

Optimization

Single Mutation, Measurable Improvement

One targeted framework mutation achieves all optimization goals — with zero structural perturbation to the CDR binding geometry.

VH FR3 N84 → Q
Deamidation Site Elimination

Asparagine at position 84 forms part of an NG motif susceptible to spontaneous deamidation in aqueous conditions. Isosteric replacement with Glutamine retains backbone geometry while removing the chemical liability.

WHY THIS POSITION IS SAFE
FR3 position 84 is outside the Kabat CDR zones and all Vernier-zone residues. N→Q is an isosteric substitution with <0.1 Å side-chain deviation — structurally invisible to the paratope.
