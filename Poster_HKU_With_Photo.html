Academic Seminar — The University of Hong Kong

Next-Generation Immune System Humanized Mice and the InSynBio AI Drug R&D Platform

Speaker: Jing Huang, Ph.D.  |  Affiliation: Genomab Biotech (www.genomab.com)  |  Venue: The University of Hong Kong  |  Date & Time: [To be confirmed]

Speaker Biography

Dr. Jing Huang received her Ph.D. in 2004 from the Institute of Zoology, Chinese Academy of Sciences. From 2004 to 2006 she conducted postdoctoral research at the Peking University Health Science Center under Academician Tanjun Tong. After 2006 she worked in the United States for 15 years at leading institutions including the Albert Einstein College of Medicine, the Aaron Diamond AIDS Research Center, The Rockefeller University, and Columbia University.

She is a member of Sigma Xi, the Genetics Society of America, and the American Aging Association, and has published more than 30 papers in journals such as Science Translational Medicine and Frontiers in Immunology. In 2019 she founded Genomab Biotech (www.genomab.com) and serves as CEO. Genomab focuses on next-generation immune system humanized animal models and AI-driven drug R&D. The company has developed dozens of immune system humanized mouse models and the InSynBio AI platform for antibody and cell therapy design, evaluation, and translational research.

Abstract

This seminar will introduce next-generation immune system humanized mice and the InSynBio AI drug R&D platform. The first part will cover the development and applications of advanced humanized mouse models (e.g., PBMC-humanized and HSC-based mice) in translational medicine, including efficacy and safety evaluation of antibody drugs (checkpoint inhibitors, bispecifics, ADCC, and autoimmune-related antibodies), oncolytic virus and TIL therapy, CAR-T for solid tumors, and tumor vaccine research. The second part will present the InSynBio AI platform, which integrates clinical and scientific data with machine learning to support AI-assisted antibody humanization, bispecific and CAR-T design, developability assessment, and dry-wet combined workflows for drug discovery and translation. The talk will highlight how next-generation humanized models and the InSynBio AI platform together support preclinical validation and AI-driven drug innovation.
